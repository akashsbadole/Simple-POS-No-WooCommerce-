## Recording Checklist (for all videos)

1. Use fresh WordPress + sample data: 8-10 products, 2 categories, 2 customers
2. Record in Chrome 1920x1080, 100% zoom, hide bookmarks bar
3. Use OBS Studio → MP4. Mic voiceover OR AI voice (ElevenLabs / CapCut)
4. Zoom on clicks (OBS zoom / Screenity). Keep mouse slow.
5. No addons enabled during recording — disable `addons/` folder
6. Export: YouTube 1080p, Shorts/Reels 1080x1920 crop of Video 1

## What NOT to Show (Addons - Skip)

Do NOT show: Advanced Reports, Customer Display, Gift Cards, Kitchen Display, Low-Stock Auto PO, Loyalty, Multi-Currency checkout, Multi-Outlet, Offline Mode, Online Ordering, Table Service, Time Clock.

Core only: Terminal, Products/Variants, Categories, Customers, Taxes, Inventory/Stock Log, Suppliers/PO, Sales History/Void, Reports (basic), Barcode Labels, Settings, Roles, Receipt Print (browser/USB/network), CSV Import/Export.
